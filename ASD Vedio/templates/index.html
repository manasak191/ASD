About the Project

AI-Powered Facial Analysis for Early Autism Identification

This project harnesses artificial intelligence to study facial expressions, gaze direction, and micro-movements to detect early behavioral signs of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). Using image and video data, our system identifies subtle patterns often missed by the human eye, enabling earlier and more accurate intervention.

By combining deep learning with advanced facial analytics, we aim to provide a non-invasive, accessible, and reliable tool that supports parents, clinicians, and caregivers in early autism screening.
